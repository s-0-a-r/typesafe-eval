# Third-party documents in this corpus

The measurement corpus contains public documents written by other people. This file lists the ones whose text is committed here, where each comes from and under which license. The manifests (`manifest_*.json`) have the exact commit, path and sha256 of every file.

PR descriptions and articles without a license that clearly covers their text are not committed. `fetch_pr_bodies.py` and `fetch_articles.py` download them from the recorded URLs and commits and check them against the sha256 in the manifests. See `.gitignore` for the list.

## Modified files

Files under `variants/` are modified versions of the documents below, made for testing: `shuffled`, `no_headings`, `padded`, `removed_middle`, `swap_sections` and `unrelated_addition` are generated by `build_pairs.py`, and `paraphrase.md` is a reworded copy written by Claude (Anthropic) on 2026-09-28. They are not the authors' text and do not represent the authors' views. The `before.md` and `after.md` files are unmodified apart from line endings (normalized to LF).
